Getting good reads

  1. Mount the phone on a tripod or shelf 50–80 cm from the board, off to one side and a little below, so each dart shows as a clear line rather than end-on.
  2. Keep it still. Calibration is tied to the exact framing. If the phone moves, snap a new empty board and re-check the four points.
  3. Light the board evenly. Two lamps from the sides cut the dart shadows that confuse detection.
  4. Calibrate on the double ring. Tap the outer edge of the double ring in the middle of 20, 6, 3 and 11. Drag any point until the chalk lines sit on the wires.
  5. Check every dart. Detection is a best guess. Tap × on a wrong dart and re-enter it with the keypad or the board.
